Welcome

Oğuz Açıkel is a student leader and the founder of AMUNAP. He serves as EU Officer at Cephok Consortium and holds the position of Under-Secretary-General across multiple Model United Nations conferences.

He is currently the Secretary-General of Pentamun Chapter One. His work focuses on building platforms for youth engagement, coordinating European projects, and leading high-level MUN simulations.

He studies Mathematics at Keçiören BİLSEM and is a 10th-grade student at Bahçelievler Anatolian High School. He speaks Turkish (Native), English (C1), and Russian (A2).
